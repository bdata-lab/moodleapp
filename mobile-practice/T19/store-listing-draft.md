# App Store & Google Play Listing Draft

**App Title**
BDATA LMS

**Short Description**
The official mobile learning app for BDATA students. Access courses, track grades, and learn on the go.

**Full Description**
Welcome to the BDATA LMS app, your dedicated mobile companion for learning! Designed exclusively for BDATA students, this app allows you to stay connected with your educational journey anytime, anywhere.

Key Features:
* **Learn Offline:** Download course materials, SCORM packages, and resources to continue learning without an internet connection.
* **Track Your Progress:** Check your grades, assignment feedback, and course completion status natively within the app.
* **Stay Connected:** Engage with teachers and peers through integrated messaging and discussion forums.
* **Complete Activities:** Submit assignments, take quizzes, and mark your attendance directly from your mobile device.
* **Instant Updates:** Receive push notifications for upcoming deadlines, new messages, and important course announcements.

*Note: This application requires an active BDATA student account to log in and access course contents.*
